# Level Design Specifications (All 36 Levels)

## Mode 1: Escape
- **Level 1 (The Gateway)**: Square arena, $140\text{ px}$ opening, 10 balls escape target. $\star\star\star = 1\text{ attempt}$.
- **Level 2 (Precision Arc)**: Circular arena, $90\text{ px}$ opening, 15 balls escape target.
- **Level 3 (Rotating Prism)**: Triangular arena rotating at $0.45\text{ rad/s}$, 20 balls escape target.
- **Level 4 (Shot Pressure)**: Pentagon arena, 20 balls escape target, strictly $\le 3\text{ shots}$.
- **Level 5 (Oscillating Heart)**: Heart arena, moving exit gate ($1.0\text{ rad/s}$) + rotating center obstacle.
- **Level 6 (Quantum Diamond)**: Diamond arena, rotating chamber ($0.6\text{ rad/s}$) + moving bar + 30 balls under $45\text{ s}$.

## Mode 2: Neon Maze
- **Level 1 (Dual Orbit)**: 2 concentric rings, static wide gates, reach center core.
- **Level 2 (Triple Gate)**: 3 concentric rings, offset gates ($0.9\text{ rad}$).
- **Level 3 (Synchronized Spin)**: 3 rings rotating in unison at $0.5\text{ rad/s}$.
- **Level 4 (Counter-Current)**: 3 rings with alternating counter-rotation ($+0.6 / -0.8 / +0.6\text{ rad/s}$).
- **Level 5 (Quantum Labyrinth)**: 4 concentric layers with high-speed counter-rotation.
- **Level 6 (The Singularity)**: 4 counter-rotating rings + central spinning obstacle + $30\text{ s}$ timer.

## Mode 3: Neon Impact
- **Level 1 (First Light)**: Square arena, 20 impacts target.
- **Level 2 (Circular Kaleidoscope)**: Circle arena, 30 impacts target.
- **Level 3 (Hexagonal Prism)**: Hexagon arena + central rotating reflector bar, 40 impacts.
- **Level 4 (Prismatic Ricochet)**: Triangle arena, high combos, 50 impacts.
- **Level 5 (Pentagram Weaver)**: Pentagon arena + twin rotating bars, 60 impacts.
- **Level 6 (Laser Symphony)**: Diamond arena + rotating cross, 75 impacts grand tapestry.

## Mode 4: Evolution vs Spikes
- **Level 1 (First Hazard)**: Square arena + 1 central spike, 15 rebounds (Tier 1 Evolution).
- **Level 2 (Twin Fangs)**: Circle arena + 2 stationary spikes, 20 rebounds (Tier 2 Glow).
- **Level 3 (Oscillating Blades)**: Triangle arena + 2 moving spikes oscillating vertically, 25 rebounds.
- **Level 4 (Pentagonal Gauntlet)**: Pentagon arena + 3 rotating spike hazards, 30 rebounds (Tier 3 Supernova).
- **Level 5 (Spinning Crucible)**: Rotating diamond arena ($0.5\text{ rad/s}$) + 4 perimeter spikes, 35 rebounds.
- **Level 6 (Supernova Genesis)**: Heart arena + 2 orbiting spikes, 40 rebounds legendary milestone.

## Mode 5: Every Contact = New Ball
- **Level 1 (Genesis Spark)**: Square arena, 2 initial balls $\to$ spawn 15 balls.
- **Level 2 (Rapid Multiplication)**: Circle arena, 2 balls $\to$ spawn 25 balls under $30\text{ s}$.
- **Level 3 (Hexagonal Cascade)**: Hexagon arena, 3 balls $\to$ spawn 35 balls.
- **Level 4 (Kinetic Accelerator)**: Pentagon arena + rotating reflector bar $\to$ spawn 45 balls.
- **Level 5 (Diamond Fusion)**: Diamond arena, 4 balls $\to$ spawn 60 balls.
- **Level 6 (Supercluster Frenzy)**: Heart arena + twin rotating obstacles $\to$ spawn 80 balls under $35\text{ s}$.

## Mode 6: Last Ball Wins
- **Level 1 (Gladiator Duel)**: Square arena, 1v1 duel against 3-life enemy.
- **Level 2 (Triangle Skirmish)**: Circle arena, 1v2 Free-For-All.
- **Level 3 (Hexagonal Melee)**: Hexagon arena, 1v3 Free-For-All.
- **Level 4 (Hazard Arena)**: Diamond arena, 1v3 with central rotating hazard bar.
- **Level 5 (Pentagon Chaos)**: Rotating pentagon arena, 1v4 melee.
- **Level 6 (The Overlord)**: Heart arena Boss Battle against 5-shield Overlord with aggressive AI thrusters.
