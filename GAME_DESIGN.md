# Game Design Document: SatisfyBall

## 1. Core Pillar: "I Almost Made It"
The emotional hook of SatisfyBall lies in the micro-adjustments of angle and power. The player sees the simulated outcome immediately, feels the kinetic momentum, and can retry within 1 tap with zero menu latency.

## 2. Modes & Gameplay Metagame

| Mode | Core Rule | Visual Aesthetic | Progression Curve |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Escape** | Guide balls through geometric openings | Cyan Glow `#00f0ff` | Static openings $\to$ Rotating shapes $\to$ Shot pressure $\to$ Moving gates |
| **Neon Maze** | Penetrate concentric rotating rings | Violet Glow `#a855f7` | Static concentric gates $\to$ Alternating counter-rotation $\to$ Multi-tier labyrinth |
| **Neon Impact** | Collisions weave persistent laser web art | Magenta Glow `#ff007f` | Reach impact milestones $\to$ High-combo ricochets $\to$ Dense geometric tapestry |
| **Evolution vs Spikes** | Safe rebounds evolve the sphere while avoiding lethal spikes | Emerald Glow `#10b981` | 1 Spike $\to$ Moving hazards $\to$ 4-tier visual evolution (Supernova) |
| **Every Contact = New Ball** | Sphere collisions trigger kinetic offspring creation | Gold Glow `#facc15` | Controlled chain reactions $\to$ Population limits $\to$ Frenzy under timer |
| **Last Ball Wins** | Gladiatorial combat physics with shields/lives against AI | Amber Glow `#ff7700` | 1v1 duel $\to$ Free-for-all $\to$ Hazard arena $\to$ Overlord Boss Fight |

---

## 3. 3-Star Rating Philosophy
- **1 Star ($\star$)**: Baseline completion of the objective.
- **2 Stars ($\star\star$)**: Skilled execution (e.g. $\le 3$ attempts, higher rebounds, fast time).
- **3 Stars ($\star\star\star$)**: Mastery threshold (e.g. 1-shot launch, flawless survival, peak time).
